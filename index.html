Human interaction · Simulation · Intelligent driving

Shuo Zhang

PhD Candidate · New York University

I build human-in-the-loop driving simulations to study how people interact with vehicles and each other in complex road environments.

My work spans research and system development: building VR interfaces and simulation tools, connecting multimodal data streams, and studying behavior, trajectory prediction, and autonomous driving policies.

DuoRoad

Bringing drivers and VR workers into one shared road environment

A dual-participant simulation platform for studying driver–worker interaction in safety-critical road scenarios, with human motion, gaze, vehicle sensing, and CARLA–SUMO co-simulation.

WZDrive

Learning driving trajectories in roadway work zones

Human-in-the-loop driving data and trajectory prediction in work zones. The dataset captures 1,183 trajectories from 33 drivers across six scenario conditions and three work-zone configurations.

WZ-Plan

A work-zone testbed for reinforcement learning-based motion planning

A configurable CARLA–SUMO testbed with six scenario families for training and evaluating a PPO motion planner with a structured spatial-temporal scene encoder.
