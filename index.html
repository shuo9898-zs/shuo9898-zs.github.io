Shuo Zhang

PhD Candidate · New York University

I am a PhD candidate in Transportation Systems at New York University (NYU). My research uses human-in-the-loop simulation to address out-of-distribution (OOD) and safety-critical scenarios in autonomous driving and road safety. Previously, I earned my master’s degree at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA).
